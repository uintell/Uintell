# Private Research Notebook

A personal knowledge platform should feel closer to a research notebook than a public encyclopedia clone.

## Notes and collections

Notes capture synthesis.
Collections capture intent.
Together they turn a pile of imported sources into a reusable operating system.

## Backlinks

Backlinks matter because they reveal how often a concept is referenced by other documents.
This often surfaces useful reading paths that keyword search alone would not reveal.
