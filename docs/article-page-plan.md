# Article Page Plan

## Target Feel

The article page should feel like a calm technical reading environment with a competent research assistant attached to it, not a dashboard with content inside it.

## Components To Improve

- document reader layout
- content renderer
- table of contents
- ask-this-page panel
- related pages and backlinks presentation

## This Sprint Changes

- strengthen article typography and spacing rhythm
- improve section hierarchy and TOC usefulness
- make code blocks more polished and copy-friendly
- simplify the sticky rail around TOC and source context
- move exploration surfaces into a clearer “continue reading” area
- make ask-page answers feel more integrated and evidence-first

## This Sprint Will Not Change

- backend architecture
- non-reader product surfaces
- optional infrastructure
- frozen legacy paths
- broad search or import workflows outside what directly helps the page

## World-Class Enough For Now

The page is good enough for this sprint when:

- the article body is clearly the emotional center of the screen
- the TOC is genuinely useful on long pages
- the AI panel helps without dominating
- evidence is easy to inspect and jump back into the page
- related pages and backlinks make continued exploration feel natural
- the reader code is easier to understand than before
